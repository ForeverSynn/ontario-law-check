# My business checklist (example)

Copy this to `CHECKLIST.md` beside `law.py` and fill it in. The skill reads it when you ask it to check a document. It's in `.gitignore` so your real one doesn't get committed by accident.

Research aid, not legal advice. Pull the current text with the tool before relying on any line.

## About the business
- Type: sole proprietor / partnership / corporation
- Where: city, Ontario (municipality matters for licensing by-laws)
- Clients: consumers, businesses, or both
- Where deals get made: client's home, my place of business, online, phone, email
- HST registered: yes / no
- Employees: none / some (ESA, WSIB)
- Address rule: e.g. "home address goes on client agreements only, never on the public website"

## Checklist
One line per topic. Start each with a bold title so the assistant can search for it. Cite sections, say what you do about it, and mark anything unsure as INTERPRETATION or LAWYER REVIEW.

- **Client type.** CPA s. 1 ("consumer"), s. 2(1). What I do: ...
- **Direct (in-home) agreements.** CPA ss. 41-43; O. Reg. 17/05 ss. 34-35. What I do: ...
- **Online and email deals.** CPA ss. 37-40; O. Reg. 17/05 ss. 31-33. What I do: ...
- **Commercial emails (CASL).** CASL ss. 6, 10, 11; SOR/2012-36 ss. 2-3. What I do: ...
- **Client data (PIPEDA).** PIPEDA Sch. 1; ss. 10.1-10.3. What I do: ...
