# Not legal advice

This tool fetches law text from the Ontario and federal government websites and points out things that might affect it. That's all it does.

- It is not a lawyer and it doesn't give legal advice. Nothing it prints, and nothing an AI assistant says while using it, is legal advice.
- Its output is not an official copy of any law. It reformats the text it downloads. In Ontario, official copies are the ones section 35 of the Legislation Act, 2006 describes (King's Printer copies and e-Laws copies in the prescribed formats). Federally, the consolidations the Minister of Justice publishes are evidence of the law (Legislation Revision and Consolidation Act, s. 31). Check anything important against those.
- It can be wrong. The sites change their layout, the parser can miss things, and the cache can be up to 3 hours old.
- It can't see case law, court decisions, regulator guidance or how the law is enforced in practice. Any of those can change the answer.
- Using it doesn't create any relationship between you and anyone who wrote or contributed to it.

If money, a dispute, a deadline, your licence or anyone's safety depends on the answer, talk to a lawyer licensed in Ontario. The [Law Society of Ontario Referral Service](https://lso.ca/public-resources/finding-a-lawyer-or-paralegal/law-society-referral-service) can get you a free 30-minute consult.
